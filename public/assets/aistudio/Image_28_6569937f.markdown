## Brand & Style

This design system embodies a disciplined, editorial minimalism tailored for high-efficiency event organizers and modern attendees. The visual tone is utilitarian, calm, and deliberate, treating interface density and data clarity with equal precision. By relying on an unyielding black-and-white tonal architecture, it strips away cognitive clutter, allowing event logistics, attendee metrics, and production timelines to take center stage.

The design movement merges Nordic functionalism with modern brutalist-leaning minimalism: heavy, pure white canvas backdrops punctuated by soft architectural surface containers (`#F5F5F7`), bold near-black pill controls (`#0B0B0B`), and expressive geometric typography. Chromatic color is strictly treated as a scarce utility—reserved exclusively for vital real-time status warnings, live capacities, and actionable system alerts.

## Colors

The system uses an uncompromising monochrome scale supported by a singular semantic accent.

- **Primary (`#0B0B0B`)**: Represents the absolute foreground anchor. Applied to primary pill buttons, selected navigation icons, solid vertical bar containers, typography headlines, and single-stroke chart lines.
- **Secondary (`#71717A`)**: Applied to supporting metadata, secondary metrics, form labels, timestamps, and inactive iconography. Ensures WCAG AA compliance against both pure white and light grey backgrounds.
- **Neutral (`#F5F5F7`)**: The structural card surface. Sits directly upon the `#FFFFFF` canvas to establish structural zones without requiring harsh outlines or dramatic shadows.
- **Accent Alert (`#EF4444`)**: The sole allowed chromatic color in the core UI. Utilized strictly for high-urgency notifications (e.g., ticket capacity ceilings, failed broadcasts, payment failures).

## Typography

Typography relies universally on `Inter` across all viewports to provide neutral, systematic clarity. 

- **Display & Metrics**: Emphasized with heavy `700` weights and tight letter spacing (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`), generating immediate visual weight in summary greeting banners and key performance indicators.
- **Hierarchy of Readability**: Headline sizes remain restrained, giving precedence to numerical data over decorative labels. Body text maintains comfortable reading height with balanced optical margins.
- **Captions & Metadata**: Rendered in muted secondary tones (`#71717A`) at `11px` to `13px` to anchor dates, capacity caps, and author signatures without competing with actionable pill components.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an asymmetrical multi-zone grid system designed for desktop dashboards and responsive collapsing.

- **Desktop (3-Zone Shell)**:
  - **Left Rail**: Fixed-width, dark floating pill capsule (72px to 80px) containing iconographic navigation.
  - **Center Column**: Fluid core space hosting personalized greetings, the upcoming highlight card, and scrollable event catalogs.
  - **Right Rail**: Dedicated utility and analytics column (320px to 380px) housing search, profile, rapid stats metrics, linear SVG chart visualizations, and account upgrades.
- **Adaptive Breakpoints**:
  - **Tablet (768px – 1024px)**: Preserves the compact icon rail; moves the right analytics rail underneath the primary event feed into a balanced 2-column container layout.
  - **Mobile (< 768px)**: Converts the dark rail into an elevated floating bottom tab bar. Stacks all dashboard modules into a single-column sequence with touch-optimized margins (`margin-mobile: 1rem`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is primarily achieved through tonal layering rather than heavy drop shadows:

- **Canvas to Card Layering**: Base canvas surfaces are pure white (`#FFFFFF`). First-order containers (stat modules, greeting blocks, list items) use solid `#F5F5F7` surfaces without visible borders.
- **Floating Controls**: Elevate select high-priority elements (such as the navigation capsule and contextual floating popovers) with an ambient, diffused shadow: `0 12px 32px -8px rgba(11, 11, 11, 0.08)`.
- **Card Hover States**: Active list cards and action modules translate slightly along the Y-axis (-2px) while transitioning to a soft `#FFFFFF` elevated fill supported by a hairline border (`#E5E5EA`).

## Shapes

The design system embraces an exaggerated pill-shaped geometry (`roundedness: 3`).

- **Containers & Shells**: Large content cards and modular surface containers utilize `rounded-2xl` to `rounded-3xl` radii (`20px` to `24px`), softening the structural grid.
- **Controls & Buttons**: Action buttons, search inputs, active selection pills, and badge indicators use fully rounded capsular profiles (`9999px`).
- **Data Elements**: Mini circular progress indicators, dot markers on continuous line graphs, and avatar frames follow absolute circular geometry, harmonizing with pill buttons across all scales.

## Components

- **Buttons**:
  - *Primary*: Solid near-black fill (`#0B0B0B`), crisp white typography (`#FFFFFF`), full pill curvature (`rounded-full`), padded with `space-sm` vertically and `space-lg` horizontally.
  - *Secondary / Utility*: Circular icon buttons with faint outlines (`#E5E5EA`) on white backdrops for directional steps and pagination.
- **Status Chips & Pills**:
  - Miniature capsular tags (`rounded-full`) with light neutral fills (`#E5E5EA` or `#FFFFFF`).
  - Active notifications include an absolute red dot marker (`#EF4444`) anchored to the top-right corner.
- **Inputs & Search**:
  - Clean capsule-shaped fields with `#F5F5F7` background, unbordered in resting states, switching to a precise 1px `#0B0B0B` stroke on active focus.
- **Cards & Lists**:
  - Dense list rows nested within primary feeds use `#F5F5F7` fills, containing thumbnail blocks, structured dual-line typography, and trailing pill CTAs ("View course" / "Manage").
- **Data Visualizations**:
  - Smooth continuous spline charts using an unaliased `#0B0B0B` stroke, accompanied by hollow or solid circular anchor dots and muted coordinate labels.
  - Circular progress gauges positioned adjacent to critical stats, featuring high-contrast progress tracks.
